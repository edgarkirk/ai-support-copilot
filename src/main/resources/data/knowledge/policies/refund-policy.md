# Refund Policy

Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2026-01-15

## Standard refund window

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date. The request must reference a valid
order and the payment must have settled successfully.

## Requests after 30 days

Requests received after 30 days are outside the standard refund window. Support agents must not promise a refund. An
exception may be escalated for manager review when there is evidence of duplicate charging, a confirmed service outage
that prevented use of the purchased service, or a documented billing error.

Being unused, forgetting to cancel, or contacting support late does not by itself qualify for an exception.

## Agent response

Explain the 30-day policy, verify the order date and payment status, and record the reason for the request. If an
exception criterion is present, escalate the case with supporting evidence.

## Evidence

The ticket should include order identifier, purchase date, customer request date, payment status, and any incident or
billing reference.
